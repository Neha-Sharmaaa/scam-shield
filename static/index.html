Meet Gena

Your parents' call assistant.

Gena picks up every unknown call, stays calm, and never lets a stranger rush Papa.

  • 🗣️ Speaks 7 languages: हिन्दी, English, ಕನ್ನಡ, தமிழ், తెలుగు, मराठी, বাংলা. She replies in whatever language the caller speaks.
  • 🤐 Gives nothing away: she never confirms personal details and never accuses anyone.
  • 📵 Never hands over the phone: unknown callers never reach Papa directly.
  • 🔁 Checks "relatives" safely: the family is told to call back on a number they already have.
  • 👨‍👩‍👦 Keeps you in the loop: an instant alert, with the reasons and the full transcript.
The problem

They all work the same way: create panic, keep the victim on the line, and get them to pay or share a code before they can ask anyone.

How it works

Gena: a polite gatekeeper that never hands over the phone.

No LLM improvising on the call: a fixed, calm script and transparent rules, so every flag comes with a reason the family can read.

📲

Gena answers first

Only unknown numbers go to Gena. Saved contacts ring straight through, with no questions.

🗣️

Asks 3 questions

Who? From where? Why? Gena greets in your family's language, then replies in whatever language the caller speaks: 7 languages, voiced by Gnani Timbre.

🔎

Checks the script

Prisma transcribes 8 kHz audio. Rules spot OTP requests, authority claims, threats, secrecy, money and remote-access demands.

🔁

"Family" gets a call-back

A "grandson in trouble" from a new number? Family claim + money or panic ends the call. Otherwise you're asked to call back on the number you already have.

🟢 Low risk

Gena takes a message and reads it to Papa in the family's own language.

🟡 Unclear

The caller is told the family will call back. You decide.

🔴 High risk

Gena ends the call politely. You get an instant alert with reasons and the full transcript.

Built on Gnani AI

Indian-language voice AI, end to end.

Speech-to-text

🎙️ Gnani Prisma v3.0

Transcribes callers from 8 kHz mono phone-band audio, even with fan/TV noise, and detects their language (indic-auto): Hinglish, Hindi, English, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Bengali. codemix keeps "OTP" and "CBI" in Latin script for reliable matching.

Text-to-speech

🔊 Gnani Timbre v3.0

Gena's voice in the caller's language, and safe messages read to Papa in the family's own language, whatever language the caller used. Every fixed line is cached, so repeat calls cost no credits.

🛡️ Scam Shield flags suspicious calls; it doesn't claim to stop every scam. When it's wrong, a genuine caller just leaves a message.
All demo data is synthetic: numbers are invalid by design and every call is scripted role-play.
