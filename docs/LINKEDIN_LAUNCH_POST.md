"CBI se bol raha hoon." "Aapka parcel customs mein pakda gaya." "Bas OTP bata dijiye." 📵
Scams like these reach our parents every single day, and they work by talking to them directly.

So I built **Scam Shield** 🛡️ for the **Great Indian AI Internship Challenge** by **Gnani AI**.

Meet **Gena**, an AI assistant that answers unknown calls *before* Papa does:

🎙️ She asks who's calling, from where, and why, on noisy 8 kHz phone audio (**Gnani Prisma**)
🌐 She replies in the caller's own language: Hindi, English, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Bengali
🚩 She spots the scam script: OTP requests, "digital arrest" threats, secrecy, money demands
📵 Scam calls end politely, and the family gets an alert with the reasons
🔊 Safe messages are read to Papa in *his* language (**Gnani Timbre**), even if the caller spoke English

In the video: an English-speaking scammer calls a Hindi family. Gena switches to English and ends the call, and Papa
still hears genuine messages in Hindi.

It flags suspicious calls; it doesn't claim to stop every scam. If it's ever unsure, a genuine caller just leaves a
message. 🙏
